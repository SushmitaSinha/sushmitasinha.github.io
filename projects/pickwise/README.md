# PickWise

**Status:** Design proposal | **Area:** Agentic AI

## Problem
Beginner affiliate creators spend time switching between product research, graphics and publishing tools.

## Approach
A supervised workflow to research up to five products, draft a Pinterest pin and matching guide, check evidence and request approval.

## Product decisions
- Bound product research and correction attempts.
- Use authorized product photos or a text-based comparison.
- Invalidate approval whenever the content changes; publish the guide before the pin.

## Evidence and limits
Team 8 capstone canvas explicitly states design proposal, not implemented or evaluated. The 60–70+ minute baseline is retrospective, not a timed benchmark. Coach is a stretch role.

## Next evidence to attach
Build one complete content package and run the golden cases. Measure active human effort separately from runtime. Team: Aswin, Sushmita and Vinil Bandaru.
